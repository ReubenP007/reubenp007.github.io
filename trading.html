05 · SIX MONTHS IN THE MARKET

Trading

PYTHON · BYBIT V5 · BUILT, BACKTESTED, DEPLOYED LIVE

A fully autonomous algorithmic crypto-trading system, designed, built, backtested and deployed in Python on the Bybit V5 API. It includes a rigorous, data-driven evaluation of LLM-in-the-loop decision-making that led to a deliberate architecture simplification, plus ReubzEdge, a commercial market-intelligence product now in development.

12+ strategies in the bake-off
730 days of backtest window
3.25 PnL to max-drawdown ratio (backtested)
24/7 live on a Linux VPS via systemd

The LLM verdict

Direction picker

-28% PNL

Claude chose trade direction over 180 days of walk-forward backtests. Result: -28% PnL versus the rules baseline, with a 70% max drawdown.

Veto layer

-26% PNL

Claude confirmed or vetoed rule-generated signals. It vetoed too many winners and cost 26% versus the baseline.

Risk manager

-36% PNL

Claude managed open positions. It exited early on normal pullbacks and cost 36% versus the baseline.

Every configuration lost to the pure-rules baseline, so the LLM was removed from the live trading loop entirely and kept for research and tooling only. The willingness to kill your own clever idea when the data says so is the whole point. Production is deterministic rules plus notifications; nothing a model says can move money.

T-01

Bot core

Single-file Python application, about 1,000 lines, one dependency. A custom HMAC-signed Bybit V5 REST client covering klines, ticker, balances, positions, orders and leverage config.

T-02

SQUEEZE strategy

Volatility-squeeze breakout on ETHUSDT daily candles. EMA200 trend filter, ATR-based stops (1.5x SL / 3.0x TP), 5x leverage, -10% kill switch. Chosen from a 12+ strategy bake-off.

T-03

Backtest framework

Parameter sweeps, walk-forward folds, triple-barrier R-expectancy with 70/30 out-of-sample splits, and a plausibility ban list that treats too-good-to-be-true metrics as a bug signal.

T-04

Risk & execution

Multi-field balance parsing for Bybit's quirks, hard position caps, daily PnL tracking, and completed-candle-only analysis so nothing repaints.

T-05

Deployment

Runs 24/7 as a systemd service on a Vultr VPS (Ubuntu 24.04) with API IP whitelisting and Discord webhooks for the full trade lifecycle.

T-06

ReubzEdge

In development: a commercial market-intelligence dashboard. Pluggable data adapters, a multi-symbol confluence scanner, and confidence numbers driven by measured historical hit rates, never model opinion.
